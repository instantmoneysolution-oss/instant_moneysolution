Privacy Policy

Learn how Instant Money Solution handles personal information when you use our website, enquire about financial products, or communicate with us.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Privacy requests: For questions or requests about your personal information, email info@instantmoneysolution.com.

1. Who We Are

Instant Money Solution ("IMS", "we", "us", or "our") offers consultation and assistance related to Personal Loans, Overdraft facilities and other financial products through Banks and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs).

Our website is www.instantmoneysolution.com. IMS provides assistance in connecting applicants with suitable financial institutions; the relevant lender independently determines eligibility, approval, rates and terms.

2. Personal Information We May Collect

The types of information involved depend on the service you request or the communication you have with us. They may include:

  • Contact details, such as name, mobile number, email address and location.
  • Loan requirements, enquiries and application-related details.
  • Employment or business details, income, salary, existing EMI obligations and other financial details relevant to a loan enquiry.
  • Identity, income, address or supporting documents when needed for an application and provided through an appropriate channel.
  • Enquiry forms, WhatsApp messages, call and customer-support records, callback preferences and communication history.
  • Basic technical or website-use information, including cookies or analytics data, where the relevant technology is enabled.

We may receive information directly from you, through our website or communications, or from authorised referral and business channels where permitted.

3. Why We Use Information

We may use relevant information to respond to enquiries, discuss loan options, coordinate an application with an appropriate Bank or NBFC, provide service updates and support, maintain CRM records, schedule callbacks, improve service quality, prevent misuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

Where permitted, we may also communicate information about relevant products and offers. Marketing communications are subject to applicable law, platform rules and your communication preferences.

4. WhatsApp, Calls and Other Communications

We use communication channels including the WhatsApp Business Platform, telephone and email to handle enquiries and permitted service or promotional communications. These interactions may be recorded in our CRM to manage customer requests, responses, follow-ups and support.

You can opt out of promotional WhatsApp messages from IMS by replying STOP. You can also write to info@instantmoneysolution.com to request that we stop permitted promotional communications through our channels. We will respect applicable opt-out requests; service-related or legally required communications may be handled separately where allowed.

Use of WhatsApp is also subject to Meta and WhatsApp's applicable terms and privacy practices.

5. Sharing Information

Where necessary and permitted for the purposes described above, information may be shared with relevant Banks and NBFCs, technology vendors providing our CRM, hosting, communications or other operational services, professional advisers, and regulators or authorities where legally required.

Relevant financial institutions and service providers may process information under their own privacy notices or contractual obligations, as applicable. We do not claim that submitting an enquiry guarantees any financial product or approval.

6. Information Security

We seek to use appropriate administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Do not send passwords, UPI PINs or one-time passwords (OTPs) to anyone claiming to represent IMS.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for periods reasonably necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, resolve enquiries, maintain appropriate records or satisfy applicable legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the relevant purpose. We review deletion and correction requests in light of applicable obligations.

8. Your Choices, Access and Deletion Requests

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about your personal data, ask us to correct inaccurate details, request deletion of eligible information, withdraw consent where applicable or raise a privacy grievance.

To make a request, email info@instantmoneysolution.com with a description of your request and sufficient information for us to verify that it relates to you. We may ask for limited additional verification before making changes. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

A request to stop marketing is different from a request to delete personal data; certain information may still need to be retained for legal or recordkeeping reasons.

9. Cookies and Third-Party Links

Our website may use cookies or comparable tools to support functionality, performance or analytics if enabled. You may manage cookies through your browser settings, subject to the features required to operate the site.

Our website may link to third-party sites, including those of financial institutions. We are not responsible for their independent privacy practices; please review their published notices before sharing information.

10. Children's Privacy

Our loan consultation services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly provide these services to children.

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, information-handling practices or applicable requirements change. The date at the top shows when this version was last updated.
