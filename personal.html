A Personal Loan is an unsecured loan provided by banks and financial institutions to meet personal exigencies.

You may consider a personal loan when you need money for an important personal expense and do not have enough savings to cover it. It can be useful for expenses such as medical emergencies, education, home renovation, weddings, debt consolidation, or other planned financial needs. Before taking a loan, make sure you can comfortably afford the monthly EMI and understand the interest rate and repayment terms.

- Quick Access to Funds: Get financial support when you need it for various personal expenses.
- No Collateral Required: Personal loans are generally unsecured, so you usually don't need to pledge any asset.
- Flexible Usage: Use the loan amount for eligible personal needs such as medical expenses, education, home renovation, weddings, or debt consolidation.
- Flexible Usage: Repay the loan through fixed monthly EMIs over a chosen tenure.
- Flexible Loan Tenure: Choose a repayment period that suits your financial situation, subject to the lender's terms.
- Simple Application Process: The application and documentation process can be convenient when you meet the lender's eligibility requirements.

Required documents typically include proof of identity (KYC), proof of income, and other financial documents if you're coming for Balance transfer. The specific documents required will be outlined in the loan application checklist provided to you.

Applying for a personal loan through an authorised channel partner can make the loan process easier and more convenient. An authorised partner can help you understand available loan options, eligibility criteria, documentation requirements, and the application process.
They can also assist with submitting your application to the appropriate lender and help you stay updated throughout the process. With professional guidance and support, you can make a more informed decision and complete your loan application with greater convenience.

Your personal loan eligibility mainly depends on:
Income– How much you earn every month.
Credit Score– Your history of repaying loans and credit cards.
Age – Your age at the time of applying for the loan.
Job or Business – Your employment or business stability.
Existing EMIs – How many loans or EMIs you are already paying.
Repayment History – Whether you have paid previous EMIs on time.
Loan Amount – How much money you want to borrow.
Employment History – How long you have been working with your current employer or running your business.
